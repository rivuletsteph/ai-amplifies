      Data sources

      Land cover: WMS layer NLCD_2021_Land_Cover_L48 at https://www.mrlc.gov/geoserver/mrlc_display/wms (MRLC). The developed / undeveloped view reads each tile’s NLCD class colours back and groups codes 21–24 against everything else; no reclassified dataset is involved.

      Rainfall: NWS MRMS radar-only QPE from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive tile service https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/cache/tile.py/1.0.0/, layers mrms::p72h-202609041200 + mrms::p24h-202609011200, decoded with IEM’s mrms_p72h / mrms_p24h value table. Window matches the NWS Lake Charles Edouard storm-total summary (issued 9:09 AM CDT Sep 4).

      Gauge totals: NWS post-storm reports for Tropical Storm Edouard, from the two offices that cover this area — Lake Charles (issued 2:10 PM CDT Sep 8, 2026, PSHLCH_2026AL05_Edouard_Rainfall.csv) for Hardin, Jefferson, Orange and Tyler, and Houston/Galveston (issued 11:45 AM CDT Sep 4, 2026, PSHHGX_2026AL05_Edouard_Rainfall.csv) for Polk. All 128 stations are full storm totals, none flagged incomplete or estimated. The NWS notes the data are preliminary and subject to correction.

      Big Thicket: NPS Land Resources Division boundary service, UNIT_CODE = 'BITH'

      Rivers: https://basemap.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSHydroCached/MapServer — USGS's cached rendering of the National Hydrography Dataset.

      Counties: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb, State_County layer 1

      Gauge: NOAA National Water Prediction Service (KOUT2) / USGS 08041500

      Basemaps: USGS The National Map (topographic, aerial); Esri World Light Gray Canvas (plain). Blank draws no basemap at all.
